Welcome to RingMe

Your to-do list and Google Calendar in one place, plus a phone call when it's time to do something.

  • ✍️Write a task and it goes straight into Google Calendar.
  • 📅Your calendar events show up here as tasks.
  • 📞When it's time, your phone rings and tells you what to do.

Connect Google Calendar

RingMe asks Google for permission to see your events and add the tasks you write. You only do this once.

How do I get a Client ID? (free, about 10 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account. No credit card is needed.
  2. At the top, make a New project called RingMe.
  3. Go to APIs & Services → Library, search Google Calendar API, and tap Enable.
  4. Go to OAuth consent screen: name it RingMe, choose External, and add your own Gmail under Test users.
  5. Go to Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID and choose Web application.
  6. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add this address:
  7. Tap Create, copy the Client ID, and paste it in the box above.

Bonus: add tasks by talking to Claude

Optional. If Claude is connected to your Google Calendar, anything you ask Claude to schedule lands in your calendar, and RingMe picks it up and calls you.

  1. Install the free Claude app (Play Store or App Store) and sign in.
  2. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors, find Google Calendar, and tap Connect. Use the same Google account as RingMe. If your phone app doesn't show it, do this once at claude.ai on a computer.
  3. In a chat, say something like:
    "Add 'Take vitamins' to my calendar today at 6 pm."
  4. Open RingMe and tap Sync. The task shows up, and RingMe calls you at 6 pm.
